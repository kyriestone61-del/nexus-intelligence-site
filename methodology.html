The process

From a conversation to a practical handoff.

  1. 1

    Start with a free consultation

    Discuss one recurring workflow, its owner and the evidence available. Identify the problem before choosing a tool.

  2. 2

    Review your Basic Diagnosis report

    Read a concise account of the facts, assumptions and unknowns. Decide whether to stop here or continue.

  3. 3

    Agree the paid engagement

    Scope the Full Diagnosis and implementation together. Confirm deliverables, price, responsibilities and acceptance criteria in writing before paid work begins.

  4. 4

    Prepare, quality check and release

    Develop the Full Diagnosis using authorized evidence. Review it through QA, resolve issues and explicitly release the report before treating it as client-ready.

  5. 5

    Implement and test

    Configure the agreed workflow, test normal use and relevant exceptions, and review the result with the people who will use it.

  6. 6

    Accept, hand over and support

    Confirm implementation acceptance against the agreed criteria. Provide the practical handoff and support defined in the engagement.

Two distinct checks

A released report. An accepted implementation.

Report QA and explicit release confirm the diagnosis is ready to share. Implementation acceptance confirms the delivered workflow meets the criteria agreed with you. One does not replace the other.

Throughout the engagement

Keep people in control.

Use authorized evidence

Agree what information is needed and who may access it. Separate observations from assumptions.

Make changes deliberately

Keep decisions with accountable people. Agree changes to scope before adding work.

Be honest about results

Define useful measures and document what is observed. Examples and estimates are not guaranteed outcomes.
