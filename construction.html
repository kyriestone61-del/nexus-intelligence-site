What makes a construction workflow a strong candidate?

The strongest opportunities are usually repetitive, digitally accessible, measurable, and owned by someone who can approve operational change.

  • The same document, status, routing, or follow-up task happens repeatedly.
  • Information already exists in email, spreadsheets, project systems, PDFs, forms, or shared drives.
  • Manual handling creates visible time, delay, rework, missed follow-up, or reporting burden.
  • A project or business owner can define the process and approve controls.
  • A useful baseline can be established before implementation.
