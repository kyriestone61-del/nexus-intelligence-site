Accessibility

Accessibility Statement

Relystra aims to make this website usable by a broad range of visitors and to improve accessibility as the site evolves. This statement is not a representation of third-party certification or universal conformance.

Current practices

The site uses semantic HTML where practical, visible keyboard focus, labeled form controls, responsive layouts, readable contrast, and interactive controls intended to be usable without a mouse.

Ongoing review

Accessibility can be affected by browsers, assistive technologies, third-party services, and future site changes. Relystra intends to test material changes and remediate identified barriers within reasonable operational constraints.

Feedback

A monitored accessibility contact method will be published before commercial website-originated intake is activated. Until then, this preview does not invent a contact address that is not actively monitored.
