Documentation & Delivery Controls

The Nexus Engagement
Evidence Chain.

Nexus is designed so that material scope, access, approvals, changes, testing, results, and handoff are documented rather than left to memory. The exact records used depend on the engagement and signed agreement.

Before implementation

Define what is true before changing it.

Measurement is more defensible when the current state, assumptions, data source, ownership, and scope are recorded before the intervention.

Opportunity Record

Business problem, owner, frequency, affected process, systems, risk level, and expected business outcome.

Baseline Ledger

Current KPI, source system, time period, calculation method, known data-quality issues, and responsible client reviewer.

Assumptions Register

Economic assumptions, exclusions, dependencies, client-provided representations, third-party constraints, and uncertainty requiring validation.

Scope & Exclusion Matrix

Included workflows, deliverables, out-of-scope work, client responsibilities, third-party costs, and explicit non-goals.

Access & Data Inventory

Systems, account owner, access method, permission level, data classification, approved purposes, and removal/rotation responsibility.

Human Control Map

Who may approve, reject, override, release, execute, escalate, and disable each material AI-assisted or automated action.

During implementation

Separate requested change from accidental scope growth.

Project history should show why a change happened, who approved it, what was tested, and which version entered production.

Decision & Approval Log

Material client decisions, approvals, rejected options, responsible person, date, and supporting context.

Change Request / Change Order

Requested change, reason, scope impact, fee/timeline impact, technical risk, and approval before out-of-scope work proceeds.

Build / Configuration Log

Material configuration changes, integrations, version notes, and dependencies sufficient for handoff and troubleshooting.

Issue & Risk Log

Observed issue, severity, owner, mitigation, client decision, status, and residual risk where appropriate.

Test Plan & Results

Test cases, expected behavior, actual behavior, exceptions, failed tests, remediation, and retest history.

UAT / Acceptance Record

Client acceptance criteria, reviewer, acceptance/rejection, outstanding items, and effective go-live approval.

After launch

Measure observed change without overstating causation.

Where multiple factors may influence performance, Nexus should distinguish observed change, modeled value, and stronger causal evidence rather than imply that every improvement was caused solely by AI.

Measurement Ledger

Post-launch KPI, reconciled data source, measurement period, exceptions, adoption, assumptions, and comparison to baseline.

Value Calculation

Formula, labor-rate assumptions, third-party costs, implementation cost, recurring cost, exclusions, and sensitivity where useful.

Operations Report

Usage, exceptions, issues, changes, known limitations, and optimization recommendations within the contracted service window.

Training & SOP Record

Who was trained, materials provided, operating instructions, escalation path, and client ownership responsibilities.

Ownership & Exit Pack

Handoff materials, access-removal checklist, credential-rotation items, vendor dependencies, documentation location, and client-owned next actions.

Case-Study Authorization

Separate written permission for client name, quote, metrics, screenshots, and publication scope. No permission is assumed from project acceptance.

Evidence rule

If Nexus cannot show the source, assumption, approval, or measurement window, it should not present the number as a proven client result.

The signed agreement remains controlling. This public framework describes intended project discipline and does not create additional warranties, service levels, or contractual obligations by itself.
