Privacy Notice

Effective August 29, 2026. This notice describes the current preview website. It must be updated before activating public CRM submissions, document uploads, marketing automation, or additional tracking technologies.

1. Current data collection

At present, the public diagnostic is designed to store diagnostic responses in the visitor’s browser rather than transmit those responses to Nexus. If a visitor accepts optional analytics, the site may load HubSpot analytics technologies that collect browser/device and website-interaction information. Server and hosting providers may also process routine technical information necessary to deliver the website.

2. Categories of information

Depending on which features are active, categories may include: information voluntarily entered into inquiry or diagnostic fields; business contact information; company and role information; website interaction data; device/browser information; IP address and similar technical identifiers; consent preferences; and records relating to communications or bookings. Public forms are not intended for passwords, authentication secrets, regulated personal data, or confidential documents.

3. Purposes

Information may be used to operate and secure the website, remember consent choices, understand website usage after consent, respond to inquiries, qualify potential engagements, maintain CRM records when that feature is activated, administer contracted services, document business communications, and comply with applicable legal obligations.

4. Categories of recipients

Information may be processed by service providers used for hosting, analytics/CRM, communications, scheduling, document storage, automation, security, or professional services, but only as those functions are actually enabled. Current examples may include the website host and, after analytics consent, HubSpot. A future commercial launch should maintain a current vendor/subprocessor inventory.

5. Sale, targeted advertising, and sensitive data

Nexus does not intend to sell personal information collected through this preview website. Nexus does not request sensitive personal data through the public diagnostic. If future website functionality materially changes these practices, the notice and consent mechanisms must be updated before the new processing begins.

6. Cookies and tracking choices

Optional HubSpot analytics is configured to load only after the visitor selects “Accept” in the website consent banner. If the browser exposes a Global Privacy Control signal through the supported browser interface, the current website treats that signal as a decline for optional analytics. Browser “Do Not Track” signals are not treated as a separate universal instruction because there is no single industry-standard interpretation; the site’s consent control governs optional analytics.

7. Other parties’ collection

When optional third-party analytics is enabled, those service providers may collect technical and interaction information through their technologies as described in their own documentation and contractual terms. Nexus should not describe third-party processing more narrowly than the actual configuration.

8. Review, correction, deletion, and privacy requests

Before public submission features are activated, Nexus should publish a monitored privacy contact method. Where applicable law grants access, correction, deletion, opt-out, appeal, or other privacy rights, requests will be evaluated and handled according to the law applicable to the requester and the processing at issue. Nexus may need to verify identity and authority before acting on a request.

9. Data minimization and retention

Nexus intends to collect only information reasonably necessary for the stated business purpose and to retain information according to business need, contractual obligations, legal requirements, security needs, and documented retention practices. Retention periods should be finalized before CRM intake and document storage are activated.

10. Security

Nexus seeks reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards appropriate to the information and service, but no website, transmission method, cloud provider, or AI system can be guaranteed completely secure. Public forms should not be used to transmit credentials or sensitive documents unless a specifically approved secure workflow is provided.

11. Children

This business-to-business website is not directed to children and is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children through public lead-generation features.

12. Policy changes

Material changes will be reflected by an updated effective date and, where reasonably appropriate, a website notice or renewed consent request before materially different optional processing begins.

13. Delaware and other U.S. privacy laws

Different state privacy laws may apply depending on Nexus’s scale, visitors, activities, and data practices. Nexus should evaluate applicable thresholds and requirements as the business grows. This notice is designed as a conservative launch framework, not a representation of certified compliance with every privacy regime.

14. Contact before commercial launch

A monitored business and privacy contact method must be added before this preview is used to collect paid-client inquiries or personal information through server-side forms. The site should not invent or publish a contact address that Nexus does not actively monitor.
