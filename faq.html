FAQ

Clear answers before we start.

How does pricing work?

Starting prices are examples. Your final price, what is included, and any outside costs are written in your proposal before work starts.

How long does implementation take?

It depends on what we are building, which systems are involved, how quickly we get access and feedback, and how much testing is needed. Your proposal will show the expected schedule.

Do we have to replace our existing software?

Usually not. We look at the tools you already use first and recommend a replacement only when there is a clear reason.

What does the client need to provide?

Usually one decision-maker, someone who knows the process well, a few real examples, any approved access we need, and timely feedback during testing.

How is client data handled?

We ask only for what is needed for the work. Do not send passwords, MFA codes, payment-card data, or other highly sensitive information unless we have agreed on a safe way to handle it.

Will AI run everything automatically?

No. We decide together what AI can do on its own and where a person must review or approve the next step.

What will Relystra not automate by default?

We do not remove important human approval from high-risk decisions, bypass security controls, share credentials, or give AI unrestricted control over money or regulated decisions.

Does Relystra guarantee ROI?

No. We measure the starting point and the results we can observe, but business outcomes also depend on your team, systems, data, customers, and market conditions.

What happens after launch?

If ongoing support is part of your agreement, Relystra can monitor the workflow, review issues, make controlled improvements, and track the agreed business measures.

Is Relystra only for construction?

No. Construction is an early specialty, but the main question is whether your business has repetitive work, measurable friction, usable information, and a clear decision-maker.
