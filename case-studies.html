Founding pilot framework

No unverified result is being presented as a case study.

The first public case study will use the evidence structure below rather than an unsupported percentage or generalized promise.

SectionEvidence required
Business problemSpecific repeatable workflow constraint and responsible owner.
BaselineMeasured current-state metric, source, period, assumptions, and known data limitations.
ImplementationWhat changed, systems involved, scope, and material dependencies.
Human controlsApproval boundaries, limits, monitoring, exception handling, and override responsibility.
Observed resultPost-launch KPI evidence over a stated measurement window with material assumptions and attribution limits.
Client quotePublished only with specific client authorization.
Next expansionWhat the available evidence justified considering next.

A client result is not automatically typical. Where a result is atypical or not generally expected, Nexus should not imply otherwise.
