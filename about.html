Operating principles

What Nexus is built to protect.

Outcome-oriented

If a useful metric cannot be defined, Nexus should not pretend a build has a measurable business case.

Control-oriented

Material business decisions remain owned by authorized people, not by a model merely because automation is possible.

Ownership-oriented

Projects are structured for documentation, handoff, access removal, and ongoing client control subject to contractual and third-party rights.
