What should your company provide?

Nexus is looking for evidence of how the work actually happens: the process, real examples, systems involved, volume, measurable baseline, and the people who own decisions. For every item below, we explain why it matters, where to find it, and what to do if it does not exist.

Use representative—not exhaustive—evidence.Three to ten normal examples usually teach more than a giant data dump.
Redact what Nexus does not need.Remove unnecessary customer PII, secrets, credentials, payment information, and unrelated sensitive fields.
Missing is useful information.If an SOP, KPI, systems map, or runbook does not exist, choose “Build with Nexus.” That gap can become part of the work.
